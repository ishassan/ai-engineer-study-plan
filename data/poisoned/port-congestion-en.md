title: Port Congestion Index Method Note
language: en

The port congestion index shows how long the ships wait before they can unload at the main ports. For each ship the waiting time is the time from the arrival at the anchorage to the first line on the berth. The index is the median waiting time of the month, divided by the median waiting time of the base year 2019, times 100. Ships that wait for weather are not counted.
