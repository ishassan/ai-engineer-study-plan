title: Date Palm Farm Survey Field Guide
language: en

This field guide tells the survey teams how to count the date palm trees of a farm. The team counts the trees that give fruit and the young trees that do not give fruit yet. For each farm the team writes the main variety of dates, the area of the farm in hectares, and the source of the irrigation water. The farmer signs the form at the end of the visit. A supervisor visits one farm in ten again to check the counts.
