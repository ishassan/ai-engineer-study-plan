title: Desalination Plant Output Notes 2025
language: en

These notes describe how the monthly output of the desalination plants is measured. Each plant reports the volume of water that leaves the plant, in cubic metres, at the end of each day. The daily values are added to give the monthly output. A plant that stops for maintenance reports zero for each day of the stop, and the reason for the stop. The meters are checked two times a year by an independent inspector. When a meter fails, the plant estimates the volume from the energy that the pumps used.
