# Rubric of the grader

Write the rubric here (week 10, step 2). Then delete this line.

## The failure category

The name of the category from evals/failure_categories.csv, and what the failure is, in one or two sentences.

## Pass

The rule for a pass, as a list of conditions.

## Fail

The rule for a fail, as a list of conditions.

## Examples

At least two outputs that pass and two that fail, from your own traces. For each one: the question, the part of the output that matters, the decision and the reason.
